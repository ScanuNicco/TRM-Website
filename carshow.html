Car Show

2025 Fall Car Show

The bi-annual TRM car show is a one of the main ways we connect to our surrounding communities. This year's fall car show will be held at Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology on October 25th, 2025 from 11:00am to 2:00pm. The Fall car show is generally smaller than the spring one, and is open to students, alumni, and the general public.
