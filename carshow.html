Car Show

2025 Spring Car Show

The bi-annual TRM car show is a one of the main ways we connect to our surrounding communities. This year's car show will be held at Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology on May 10th, 2025 from 11:00am to 2:00pm. We hope this one will be on the larger side, so if you have an interesting vehicle please consider registering! We will have food trucks, music, awards, and more!
